Terms

Customers can browse without an account, add current catalog products to a cart, and submit a product-payment checkout. The backend confirms product price and stock before Paystack payment starts.

Still needed for complete terms

  • The seller's registered legal name and business contact details
  • When an order becomes accepted and when either party may cancel it
  • How pricing errors, disputes, complaints, and applicable law are handled

Privacy

Checkout asks for a name, email address, WhatsApp number, delivery address, optional order notes, and explicit WhatsApp consent. The browser stores cart and pending-checkout recovery data. Ask Kawama sends customer questions and optional product-page context to the backend and keeps its conversation identifiers in session storage.

Product payment takes place on Paystack's hosted checkout. Payment confirmation comes from the backend, not from browser callback details.

Still needed for a complete privacy notice

  • The data controller's legal identity and privacy contact
  • Purposes, legal bases, retention periods, processors, and international transfers
  • Customer rights, complaint routes, cookie details, and security incident contacts

Delivery & returns

Paystack collects the backend-confirmed product amount only. Delivery is quoted separately, paid on delivery, and coordinated after product payment. An unquoted delivery fee is not a free-delivery promise.

Delivery details still needed

  • Service areas, fee calculation, delivery estimates, and failed-delivery handling
  • Who to contact for address changes or delivery problems

Return details still needed

  • Eligibility, time limits, item condition, exclusions, and evidence requirements
  • The return process, transport costs, exchanges, and refund method and timing
